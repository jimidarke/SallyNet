Weather icons + current-conditions data: Environment and Climate Change Canada
================================================================================

The weather glyphs in this directory (00.png .. 48.png) are the official Environment
Canada City Page Weather icons (https://weather.gc.ca/weathericons/<NN>.gif), converted
from GIF to 2x PNG for the SallyNet Roku channel. They are keyed by the EC `iconCode`
(00..49) returned in the citypage XML and served to the Roku via /api/state.weather.icon.

The icons and the underlying weather data are provided by the Meteorological Service of
Canada under the Open Government Licence - Canada:
  https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada
