## Quirks worth knowing

- **460 V8 drinks fuel.** Plan for 6–8 mpg. This is not fixable, only respected.
- **Dual gas tanks with a crossed fuel system.** Switch on FRONT → engine pulls from REAR (and gauge shows front, which is wrong); switch on REAR → gauge reads rear correctly but engine stalls within a couple minutes. A previous shop replaced the pump and called it done; the real fault is upstream in the selector valve or its wiring. Plan it by trip distance off the rear tank only; ignore the gauge.
- **Power steering** — proper, not the wrestling-match kind older RVs sometimes have
- **Foot-press high-beam switch** on the driver-side floor pan
- **Factory CD player** still in the dash and still works
- **No air conditioning.** Live with it; shade and altitude are the strategy.
- **Big underfloor propane tank** feeding the furnace — see [subsystems/tanks/README.md](../subsystems/tanks/README.md) for the hands-off rule
- **Furnace works great.** **Dometic RM763 fridge is dead** — used as storage now.
- **Plumbing solid.** New sewage connector installed two years ago; new **DPHCF42-12V** water pump. **Hot water tank works great.**
- **Interior bathtub** — uncommon for a coach this size, but the RB floorplan earns its name.

## What's been bolted in over the years

She's accumulated a bit of a tech stack. The short list:

- **Pyle TV** with built-in DVD player + **AGPtek mini 1080** media player + **Roku** + **9" TFT 4-camera backup-cam head** (RCA video out) all routed through an HDMI input selector — main living-area entertainment + rolling rear vision
- **Raspberry Pi 3B+** with 2× USB controllers + **~1TB USB HDD** (exFAT), RetroPie (quietly also a Samba + MiniDLNA media server)
- **QX210-NW2** industrial 4G LTE WiFi router — the network backbone
- **ANNTLENT Cell Phone Signal Booster** (Band 2/5/12/17) for Canadian carriers, directional roof antenna on a spire mount + indoor repeater
- Rear panel with USB charge ports, voltage indicator, and **Uniden FRS** charging stations
- Recently upgraded house battery + Progressive Dynamics **PD4135KW2B** converter
